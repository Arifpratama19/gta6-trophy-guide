# GTA 6 Trophy Guide

Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide"](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01) ($250).

[![gta6-trophy-guide-01](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-trophy-guide-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-trophy-guide-01/Arifpratama19/gta6-trophy-guide/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-trophy-guide-01/Arifpratama19/gta6-trophy-guide/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)

An offline web app that is the guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a checklist saved in the browser, export and import, filters, a spoiler setting, a roadmap to the platinum, and the missable trophies flagged.
