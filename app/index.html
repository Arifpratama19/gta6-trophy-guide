Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough is enough, everything is offline, and there are no online trophies. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and estimates about 200 hours; PushSquare puts it closer to 300. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them alone.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, watch Chapter 1

    Enjoy the five chapters (roughly 60–80 hours). The only possibly missable trophy lives here: in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia to the balcony when she talks about meditating and accept. See Positive Mental Attitude below. Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars as you go — both count toward 100% later. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter; there is no mission replay.

  2. Step 2 — 100% Completion checklist

    After the final mission, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is far kinder than it sounds — closer to 60% true completion, with most side quests not required and many collectibles only partly required. You need all robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests, plus 70 of 80 challenges. Finishing it earns A Long Road and most of the remaining trophies along the way.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup

    Finish the eight qualifying stranger strands, the five character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), and any stray trophies left — the easter eggs, the tank, the plane at VCIA, the UFO. Then the Platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops.

Missable watch: Positive Mental Attitude — one meditation in Chapter 1 at Jason's house may be required and is gone after Chapter 1. Do it before Paradise Lost pops.
